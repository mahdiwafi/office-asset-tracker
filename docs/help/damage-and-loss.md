# Damage and loss

Accidents happen — equipment gets dropped, spilled on, lost, or stolen. This page explains what to do and what you are responsible for.

## Report it within 24 hours

Tell ICT about any damage, loss or theft as soon as you notice it, and always within 24 hours. Speed matters for three reasons:

- damaged equipment can be repaired while the damage is fresh and cheap to fix
- stolen equipment can be reported to the police and remotely locked while it is still traceable
- your insurance claim (see below) requires a report

You can report through the tracker (open the loan and choose "Report damage or loss") or by contacting ICT directly. You do not need to wait to be asked for details — report first, details later.

## What happens next

ICT will assess the item. Depending on the damage we will repair it, replace it, or write it off. While it is being repaired you can borrow a replacement from the spare pool, so your work does not stop.

Do not try to fix equipment yourself, and do not take it to an outside repair shop. Our insurance and warranty only cover repairs we arrange. Opening a laptop or installing non-approved parts can void the warranty entirely.

## What it costs you

Small accidents are normal and covered — a cracked screen, a spilled coffee on the keyboard, a worn-out charger. You are responsible for the cost only in cases of negligence: repeated damage to the same item, damage from deliberate misuse, damage under the influence of alcohol, or loss because the item was left unattended in a public place.

The charge is the repair cost, or the current value of the item if it cannot be repaired. Charges are deducted from your final salary payment, and you will be told the amount before it is deducted.

## Theft

If equipment is stolen, report it to the police yourself within 24 hours and give ICT the police report number. ICT will lock the device remotely if it supports it — this protects the office data on it, which is more important than the hardware. You are not charged for theft that you reported promptly and that happened in reasonable circumstances (for example a break-in at your home or a bag stolen from the office).

## Insurance

All office equipment is covered by the office contents insurance. The insurance does not cover negligence (see above), and it covers the equipment, not your personal data — back up your files regularly.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether something counts as reportable damage, report it anyway. "We thought it was too small to mention" is the most expensive sentence in this policy.
