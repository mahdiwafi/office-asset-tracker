# How to request equipment

This page explains how to ask for equipment — a laptop, a second monitor, a headset, a phone, anything on the office inventory list.

## Before you request

Check the asset list in the tracker first. Many items are shared, so you may find what you need is already available and can be borrowed immediately rather than requested. If the item exists but is currently out on loan, you can still submit a request — you will be put on the list, and we will tell you when the item is back.

## Raising a request

Open the tracker and go to "Raise a request". You will need to say:

- which item you want (choose from the list — if you are not sure, pick the closest match and describe what you need in the notes)
- when you need it by
- how long you expect to need it for

A short note explaining what the equipment is for helps the approver decide quickly. For example: "A second monitor for report writing" is better than "A monitor".

## What happens next

Your request goes to your manager first, and then to ICT. You will see the status update in the tracker as it moves: Requested, Approved, Ready to collect, Collected, or Returned. Most requests are decided within two working days. If something is unclear, the approver may send it back with a question — check the notes on the request so you can reply quickly.

## When a request is approved

You will get a notification that the item is ready. Collect it from the ICT office during working hours. You will be asked to confirm the item's condition when you take it — please look it over then and there. Once you confirm, you are responsible for it (see Taking care of equipment).

## Urgent needs

If you need something the same day — for example your laptop has failed and you need a replacement to keep working — contact ICT directly instead of raising a request. We keep a small pool of spare laptops and phones exactly for this. Emergency loans follow the same rules as normal loans, just faster.
