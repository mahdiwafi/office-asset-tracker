# Loan periods and renewals

This page explains how long you can keep equipment, how to keep it longer, and what happens if it comes back late.

## The standard loan period

The standard loan period is 14 days. Your due date is shown in the tracker next to the item — this is the date the item must be back, not the date we ask for it.

Some items have different periods: cables, dongles and headsets are usually borrowed for a few days; project equipment (projectors, cameras) is often borrowed for a single event plus a day either side. The tracker always shows the exact due date, so check the item's page.

## Renewals

You can extend a loan twice, each time by up to 14 days, as long as nobody else is waiting for the item.

To renew: open the loan in the tracker and press "Renew" before the due date. The renewal is automatic unless the item is already promised to someone else — in that case you will see "Renewal refused: reserved for another staff member" and the item must come back on time.

Renewing after the due date is not possible. If you missed the deadline, return the item and raise a new request — you will go to the back of the list.

## Long-term needs

If you need an item for more than a few months — for example a permanent docking station — do not keep renewing. Raise a request with "long-term" in the notes, and your manager can assign the item to you permanently through a formal assignment. Permanently assigned items are still tracked, and you still return them when you leave (see Returning equipment when you leave).

## Returning late

Returning equipment late is more than an inconvenience: the next person's start date, project or workshop depends on that item. If you know you will be late, tell ICT before the due date — we may be able to rearrange the next borrower. Unannounced late returns are recorded on your loan history. Repeated lateness can limit your borrowing options: you may be asked to use walk-in equipment only, or to give a deposit for high-demand items.

## Weekends and holidays

Due dates are calendar dates, not working days. A laptop due back on a Saturday is expected on Saturday. If you are travelling with the equipment over a due date, renew before you leave — renewal covers the whole period including weekends.

If you are off sick and an item is due, a colleague can return it for you. They do not need special permission; they just bring the item to the ICT office and give your name.
