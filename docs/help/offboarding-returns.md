# Returning equipment when you leave

When you leave the office — whether you resign, retire, or your contract ends — everything you borrowed must come back. This page walks you through it.

## What to return

Return every item shown as "on loan to you" in the tracker, including the obvious and the forgettable:

- laptop and its charger
- phone, and any SIM card issued for office use
- dongles, adapters, headsets, keyboards, mice
- project equipment or demo equipment borrowed for an event
- any equipment assigned to you permanently (see Loan periods and renewals)

If you are not sure whether something belongs to the office, ask. A returned item that was actually personal is no problem; a kept item that was actually office property is.

## Before you return it

Do these in order, about a week before your last day:

1. Back up your personal files (photos, personal documents) to your own storage. Office documents stay with the office — do not copy confidential work files to personal drives.
2. Delete your personal files from the device. A factory reset looks after the rest.
3. Make sure you can sign in to the tracker one last time, so you can see your loans.

ICT will wipe the device properly after you hand it back — anything you forgot to back up will be gone. There is no way to recover data from a returned device.

## How to hand it back

Book a return slot with ICT through the tracker ("Return equipment" on your loan page) or by emailing ICT. Bring everything in one visit, in the original bag or box if you still have it. Hand the items over in person; leaving equipment on a desk with a sticky note does not count as returned.

If you are working remotely on your last day, mail the equipment with tracked delivery and send ICT the tracking number. The equipment is considered returned when it reaches the office, not when you post it.

## What happens after

ICT checks each item against the tracker: condition, completeness (charger present? SIM present?), and whether the device is unlocked and wiped. You will receive a return receipt in the tracker once everything clears.

Outstanding items delay your final paperwork. If an item is damaged or missing, it is treated like any other damage or loss (see Damage and loss) — the same 24-hour reporting rule applies, so report problems at handover, not later.

## One more thing

Your office accounts (email, tracker, systems) are closed on your last working day. Make sure anything colleagues might need from you — documents, permissions, handover notes — is shared before then, because after that day you will not be able to reach them.
